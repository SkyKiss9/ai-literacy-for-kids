# Lesson 1 Teacher Packet

This packet is the fastest way to run `Lesson 1: What Is AI?` in a real classroom or family pilot.

## Packet Includes

- core lesson: `curriculum/01-what-is-ai.md`
- safety worksheet: `worksheets/my-ai-safety-rules.md`
- safety reference: `guides/child-ai-safety.md`
- trial feedback capture: `guides/classroom-trial-feedback-template.md`

## What To Prepare

- one adult-controlled AI account
- one backup source such as a dictionary, encyclopedia, or trusted website
- paper for student questions
- one printed copy of the worksheet per student, or one notebook version

## Exact Teacher Prompt

Use this prompt first:

```text
Explain what artificial intelligence is to a 9-year-old student using a simple example from everyday life.
```

## Suggested Board Plan

Write three headings:

- Learning tools
- Thinking tools
- Creative tools

Put student answers under those headings during the warm-up.

## What To Watch For

- students describing AI as magic
- students assuming AI is always correct
- students trying to ask personal or private questions
- students needing clearer examples of what AI can and cannot do

## One Good Exit Question

Ask every student to finish:

```text
AI can help me with ____, but I should remember ____.
```

## Quick Revision Targets After The Lesson

- simplify one confusing explanation
- improve one example prompt
- strengthen one privacy reminder if needed
