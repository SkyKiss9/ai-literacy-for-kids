# Lesson 6 Teacher Packet

This packet helps a teacher run `Lesson 6: Exploring Science Questions` as a low-risk inquiry lesson.

## Packet Includes

- core lesson: `curriculum/06-exploring-science-questions.md`
- science worksheet: `worksheets/science-questions.md`
- safety reference: `guides/child-ai-safety.md`
- trial feedback capture: `guides/classroom-trial-feedback-template.md`

## What To Prepare

- one adult-controlled AI account
- one everyday science topic such as shadows, plants, or water
- one simple observation students can discuss safely

## Suggested Demonstration

Use AI to explain one everyday science question, then immediately ask:

- what could we observe ourselves?
- what should we test instead of just trusting?

## What Students Should Notice

- science explanations are stronger when tied to observation
- AI can suggest ideas, but evidence still comes from the world
- simple questions can still be checked carefully

## Good Exit Prompt

```text
To check a science answer, I can ____.
```

## Best Post-Lesson Revisions

- choose a simpler topic if students get lost
- strengthen the observation step
- note which examples led to the best evidence discussion
