# Lesson 7 Teacher Packet

This packet helps a teacher run `Lesson 7: Checking Answers With Evidence` as a practical evidence-checking lesson.

## Packet Includes

- core lesson: `curriculum/07-checking-answers-with-evidence.md`
- evidence worksheet: `worksheets/evidence-checking.md`
- safety reference: `guides/child-ai-safety.md`
- trial feedback capture: `guides/classroom-trial-feedback-template.md`

## What To Prepare

- one adult-controlled AI account
- one answer from AI
- one second source such as a book, chart, or trusted article

## Suggested Demonstration

Show students:

- what the AI answer says
- what the second source says
- which parts match
- which parts still feel uncertain

## What Students Should Notice

- confidence is not the same as proof
- stronger answers usually come from checking more than one source
- adults and trusted references still matter

## Good Exit Prompt

```text
When I want stronger evidence, I can check ____.
```

## Best Post-Lesson Revisions

- choose a more familiar comparison topic if students struggle
- improve the checklist wording
- note where students most often trusted tone over evidence
