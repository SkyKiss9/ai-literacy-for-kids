# Lesson 5 Teacher Packet

This packet helps a teacher run `Lesson 5: Writing With AI Without Copying` while keeping student ownership central.

## Packet Includes

- core lesson: `curriculum/05-writing-with-ai-without-copying.md`
- writing worksheet: `worksheets/writing-with-ai.md`
- safety reference: `guides/child-ai-safety.md`
- trial feedback capture: `guides/classroom-trial-feedback-template.md`

## What To Prepare

- one adult-controlled AI account
- notebook paper or printed worksheet
- one short writing topic students can complete in a paragraph

## Suggested Demonstration

Show only safe AI support tasks:

- brainstorming topic ideas
- turning one student idea into a simple outline
- suggesting one sentence improvement

Do not demonstrate a full AI-written essay as the model.

## What Students Should Notice

- ideas can be supported without being copied
- revision is different from replacement
- the final paragraph should still sound like the student

## Good Exit Prompt

```text
One safe way to use AI for writing is ____.
```

## Best Post-Lesson Revisions

- simplify the difference between help and copying
- improve the revision example
- note where students blurred the line between “assist” and “replace”
