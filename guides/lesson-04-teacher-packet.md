# Lesson 4 Teacher Packet

This packet helps a teacher run `Lesson 4: Reading With AI` without turning the session into summary copying.

## Packet Includes

- core lesson: `curriculum/04-reading-with-ai.md`
- reading support sheet: `worksheets/reading-with-ai.md`
- safety reference: `guides/child-ai-safety.md`
- trial feedback capture: `guides/classroom-trial-feedback-template.md`

## What To Prepare

- one short reading passage
- one adult-controlled AI account
- one question students can answer from the original text

## Suggested Demonstration

Ask AI to do only one helpful thing at first:

```text
Explain this paragraph in simpler words for a 9-year-old.
```

Then compare:

- what the original text says
- what the AI explanation changed
- what details were kept or lost

## What To Watch For

- students wanting the shortcut instead of the reading
- students copying the simplified version without comparison
- students skipping evidence from the original text

## Exit Prompt

```text
AI can help me read by ____.
```

## Best Revisions After The Lesson

- shorten the reading passage if needed
- choose a clearer paragraph for simplification
- add one stronger “read first, then ask AI” reminder
