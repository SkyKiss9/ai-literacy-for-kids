# Parent Guide

This guide helps parents introduce AI safely at home.

## Main Idea

Children should learn that AI is a helper, not a boss and not a replacement for thinking.

AI can explain, suggest, summarize, and practice with your child. But it can also be wrong, confusing, or unsuitable. Parents should guide early use.

## Good Home Uses

- Ask AI to explain a difficult word
- Practice reading comprehension
- Generate math practice questions
- Brainstorm story ideas
- Ask for a simpler explanation of a science topic
- Compare two explanations and discuss which is clearer

## Home Safety Rules

Teach your child:

- Do not enter your full name, address, phone number, school details, photos, or passwords.
- Do not ask AI for help with private family problems without telling an adult.
- Do not copy AI answers and pretend they are your own.
- Check important answers with a parent, teacher, book, or trusted source.

## A Simple Family Agreement

```text
In our family, AI is a learning helper.
We use it with honesty.
We do not share private information.
We check important answers.
We ask an adult when something feels serious or confusing.
```
