# Lesson 2 Teacher Packet

This packet helps a teacher run `Lesson 2: AI Can Help, But It Can Be Wrong` with minimal preparation.

## Packet Includes

- core lesson: `curriculum/02-ai-can-help-but-it-can-be-wrong.md`
- safety worksheet: `worksheets/checking-ai-answers.md`
- safety reference: `guides/child-ai-safety.md`
- trial feedback capture: `guides/classroom-trial-feedback-template.md`

## What To Prepare

- one adult-controlled AI account
- one factual question with a trusted answer
- one opinion question
- one book, chart, or trusted article for comparison

## Good First Factual Prompt

```text
Why do penguins not fly?
```

## Good First Comparison Question

Ask students to compare:

- what the AI answer sounds like
- what the trusted source says
- what details match
- what details feel uncertain

## Teacher Reminder

Do not try to embarrass the AI tool. The lesson is about checking, not mocking mistakes.

## Exit Prompt

```text
When AI gives an answer, I should also ____.
```

## Best Post-Lesson Revision Targets

- improve the comparison example
- simplify the checking checklist
- note what students treated as “automatically true”
