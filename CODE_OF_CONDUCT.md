# Code of Conduct

## Our Goal

This project supports educators, families, and contributors working on child-safe AI literacy. We want discussions to be practical, respectful, and safe for a public-interest education project.

## Expected Behavior

Please:

- be respectful and constructive
- focus on improving materials, safety, and clarity
- assume good intent while still reviewing critically
- welcome educators, parents, translators, and non-technical contributors
- raise child-safety concerns clearly and directly

## Unacceptable Behavior

Do not:

- harass or insult other contributors
- mock teachers, parents, or children
- post sexualized, hateful, or abusive content
- push unsafe advice for children
- derail discussion with AI hype or hostility

## Maintainer Responsibilities

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove content that harms collaboration or child safety. Repeated harmful behavior may result in blocked participation.

## Reporting

Open a private maintainer contact request through a GitHub issue only if it does not expose a child-safety risk publicly. For unsafe educational content, please also follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
