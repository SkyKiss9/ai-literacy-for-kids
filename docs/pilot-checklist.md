# Pilot Checklist

Use this checklist before saying a lesson is ready for real classroom or family testing.

## Content

- Lesson goal is explicit
- Warm-up fits the target age
- Teacher prompt is short and safe
- Reflection step exists
- Privacy reminder is explicit

## Delivery

- Adult-controlled AI account is available
- Backup source for answer-checking is ready
- Worksheet or notebook task is ready
- Timing looks realistic

## Safety

- No prompt asks for child personal data
- No activity treats AI as unquestioned truth
- Students are not asked to use unsupervised accounts
- Serious topics are redirected to adults

## Follow-up

- Feedback was recorded
- Confusing sections were identified
- At least one revision was captured
