# Lesson 8: Building My Personal AI Safety Rules

## Goal

Students summarize what they learned by creating their own age-appropriate AI safety rules.

## Time

30-45 minutes

## Materials

- [worksheets/my-ai-safety-rules.md](../worksheets/my-ai-safety-rules.md)
- Pens or pencils

## Warm-Up

Ask:

- What rules help us stay safe online?
- What rules also matter when using AI?

## Demonstration

Review the course habits:

- ask clearly
- check answers
- protect privacy
- use AI honestly
- ask an adult when something is serious

## Activity

Students write or discuss 3-5 personal AI rules. The class can combine repeated ideas into a group poster.

## Closing Reflection

Ask each student to finish:

```text
One AI rule I want to remember is ____.
```

## Teacher Notes

The point is ownership. Let students restate the rules in their own words when possible.
