# Lesson 4: Reading With AI

## Goal

Students practice using AI to support reading without letting AI replace reading.

## Time

30-45 minutes

## Materials

- A short reading passage
- Teacher-controlled AI tool

## Warm-Up

Ask:

- What do you do when a sentence is confusing?
- Can a helper explain a paragraph in simpler words?

## Demonstration

Use AI to:

- explain a hard sentence
- list three key ideas
- suggest two questions to think about

## Activity

Students read a short paragraph first. Only after that, the teacher uses AI to simplify one part and the class compares both versions.

## Safety And Learning Reminder

AI should help us understand the text, not skip the text for us.

## Reflection

Complete:

```text
AI can help me read by ____.
```

## Teacher Notes

Avoid turning the lesson into summary copying. Keep students close to the original text.
