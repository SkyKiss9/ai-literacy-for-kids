# Lesson 5: Writing With AI Without Copying

## Goal

Students learn how AI can support planning and revision without doing all the thinking for them.

## Time

30-45 minutes

## Materials

- Notebook paper
- Teacher-controlled AI tool

## Warm-Up

Ask:

- What is the difference between getting an idea and copying someone else's work?

## Demonstration

Show three useful AI supports:

- brainstorming topic ideas
- turning one idea into an outline
- suggesting ways to improve a sentence

## Activity

Students write their own short paragraph. After drafting, the teacher asks AI for revision ideas and the class chooses which suggestions are worth using.

## Honesty Reminder

Teach:

```text
AI can help me start, but my final writing should still be mine.
```

## Reflection

Complete:

```text
One safe way to use AI for writing is ____.
```

## Teacher Notes

Focus on ownership and revision. Avoid showing AI-generated full essays as the model to follow.
