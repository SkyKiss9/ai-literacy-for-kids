# Lesson 7: Checking Answers With Evidence

## Goal

Students practice using books, observation, and adult guidance to verify AI answers.

## Time

30-45 minutes

## Materials

- One answer from AI
- One second source such as a book, article, chart, or adult explanation

## Warm-Up

Ask:

- If two answers are different, how do we decide what to trust?

## Demonstration

Show one AI answer and one trusted source. Model how to compare:

- facts
- missing details
- confidence level
- source quality

## Activity

Students use a simple checklist:

- What does AI say?
- What does another source say?
- What matches?
- What does not match?
- What should we ask an adult?

## Evidence Rule

Teach:

```text
One answer is not enough when the question matters.
```

## Reflection

Complete:

```text
When I want stronger evidence, I can check ____.
```

## Teacher Notes

Use familiar examples first so students focus on the checking habit.
