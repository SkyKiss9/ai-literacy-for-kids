# Course Outline

This starter course introduces AI literacy to elementary school students through safe, practical, and curiosity-driven activities.

## Audience

- Recommended age: 7-12
- Format: classroom, parent-child learning, or small community workshop
- Lesson length: 30-45 minutes
- Required tools: teacher or parent device with an AI assistant, projector optional

## Learning Goals

By the end of the course, students should be able to:

- Explain that AI is a tool that predicts and generates answers from patterns
- Ask clearer questions
- Compare AI answers with books, observation, and adult guidance
- Use AI to support reading, writing, and curiosity
- Understand that AI can make mistakes
- Protect private information
- Use AI kindly and responsibly

## Lesson List

1. What Is AI?
2. AI Can Help, But It Can Be Wrong
3. Asking Better Questions
4. Reading With AI
5. Writing With AI Without Copying
6. Exploring Science Questions
7. Checking Answers With Evidence
8. Building My Personal AI Safety Rules

## Assessment

This course does not use exams. Suggested assessment methods:

- Student reflection cards
- Group discussion
- Teacher observation
- A final "My AI Safety Rules" worksheet
- Parent feedback after home practice

## Classroom Safety Setup

Children should not use general AI tools alone during the early lessons. A teacher or parent should operate the tool, read outputs aloud, and guide discussion.

Students should not enter names, addresses, school details, photos, passwords, or private family information into AI tools.
