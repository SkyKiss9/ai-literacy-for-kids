# Lesson 6: Exploring Science Questions

## Goal

Students use AI to generate science questions and explanations, then connect them back to observation and evidence.

## Time

30-45 minutes

## Materials

- One everyday science topic
- Teacher-controlled AI tool
- Simple observation materials if available

## Warm-Up

Ask:

- Why do shadows change?
- Why do plants lean toward light?

## Demonstration

Use AI to explain one science idea simply, then ask:

- What could we observe in real life?
- What part should we test instead of just believing?

## Activity

Students choose one science question, hear an AI explanation, and then write one observation or experiment that could help check it.

## Evidence Reminder

Science is stronger when we compare explanations with observation.

## Reflection

Complete:

```text
To check a science answer, I can ____.
```

## Teacher Notes

Keep experiments simple and safe. The lesson is about connecting AI explanations to the real world.
