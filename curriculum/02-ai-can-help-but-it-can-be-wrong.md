# Lesson 2: AI Can Help, But It Can Be Wrong

## Goal

Students learn that useful-sounding AI answers still need checking.

## Time

30-45 minutes

## Materials

- Whiteboard or paper
- Teacher-controlled AI tool
- One short factual question and one opinion question

## Warm-Up

Ask:

- Have you ever heard someone say something confidently that was wrong?
- Can a computer also sound confident and still be wrong?

## Demonstration

Ask AI a simple question that can be checked with a book or classroom knowledge.

Then ask students:

- What sounds believable?
- What should we verify?

## Activity

Give students two answers about the same topic: one from AI and one from a book or trusted source. Ask them to circle differences.

## Critical-Thinking Moment

Teach this rule:

```text
Helpful is not the same as always correct.
```

## Reflection

Complete:

```text
When AI gives an answer, I should also ____.
```

## Teacher Notes

Choose low-stakes examples. The point is not to trick students, but to normalize checking.
