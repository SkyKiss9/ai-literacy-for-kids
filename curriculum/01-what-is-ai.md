# Lesson 1: What Is AI?

## Goal

Students learn that AI is a computer tool that can generate answers, ideas, pictures, and explanations, but it does not think or understand like a person.

## Time

30-45 minutes

## Materials

- Whiteboard or paper
- Teacher or parent device with an AI assistant
- Optional projector

## Key Ideas

- AI is a tool made by people.
- AI learns patterns from large amounts of information.
- AI can help us learn, but it can also make mistakes.
- We should ask questions, check answers, and protect private information.

## Warm-Up

Ask students:

- What tools help us learn?
- How is a dictionary useful?
- How is a calculator useful?
- What do you think an AI tool can help with?

Write answers on the board. Group them into learning tools, thinking tools, and creative tools.

## Demonstration

Teacher prompt:

```text
Explain what artificial intelligence is to a 9-year-old student using a simple example from everyday life.
```

After reading the answer, ask:

- Which part was easy to understand?
- Which part was confusing?
- What question should we ask next?

## Activity

Students work in pairs and write one question they would like to ask AI.

The teacher chooses 2-3 questions and asks AI in front of the class.

For each answer, the class discusses:

- Did the answer make sense?
- What did we learn?
- What should we check with a book, teacher, parent, or observation?

## Safety Moment

Teach this rule:

```text
I do not put private information into AI.
```

Examples of private information:

- Full name
- Home address
- Phone number
- Passwords
- School schedule
- Family problems
- Photos of myself or classmates

## Reflection

Students complete this sentence:

```text
AI can help me with ____, but I should remember ____.
```

## Teacher Notes

Avoid presenting AI as magic. Keep the framing simple: AI is a powerful tool that can help with words, ideas, and explanations, but it needs human judgment.
